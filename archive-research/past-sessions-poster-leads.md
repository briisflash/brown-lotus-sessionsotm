## Editorial checklist before creating each past-session record

- Confirm event actually happened
- Confirm precise date including year and location
- Verify the event title and artist lineup
- Choose a final poster rather than a discarded design variant
- Check image use / attribution and public display permissions
- If available, add event photos, performance recordings, interviews, setlists and credits
- Cross-link to artist profiles
